Rates sourced from FNB published rate sheet dated 19 May 2026. The 48hr Cash Accelerator rate applied in this model is 6.55% nominal (NACM) — the rate applicable to the R1M–R4.99M balance tier, which corresponds to Maru's projected investable balances at 90%. The advertised maximum of 6.90% applies only to balances of R50M+. All other product rates are maximum advertised rates and may be subject to tiering. Interest is calculated monthly using ((1 + r)1/12 − 1) applied to the investable portion of each month's closing balance, sourced from Maru's internal cashflow forecast. Actual interest will depend on timing of deposits, applicable tier, and product terms. This document is prepared for internal decision-making purposes and does not constitute financial advice.

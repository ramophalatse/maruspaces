The Property

Built on the one ridge in the city the cloud can't clear.

Most mornings, Sanctuary Mandela sits above the inversion line while the rest of Johannesburg wakes up underneath it. Maru Spaces restored the original 1920s brickwork and roofline, then built two new wings in the same red face-brick — quiet enough that, from the ground, you can't tell which walls are a century old.
